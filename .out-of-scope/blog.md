# Blog / Writing Section

This site has no blog, writing or resources section: no posts collection, no `/writing` routes, no RSS feed.

## Why this is out of scope

The site is a consulting portfolio. Each page exists to move a visitor
towards a conversation: home, about, work, audit, contact. All of its
content is written once and kept current along with the offer it describes.

A blog works differently. It only pays off with a steady publishing cadence,
and nobody maintains one for this site. An empty or stale blog does more
harm than having none: a "latest post" from a year ago tells a prospective
client the business has gone quiet, which undermines the portfolio pages
that are actually doing the work.

Indexable content and proof of expertise are better served by what the
site already has: the case studies in `src/content/case-studies/` and the
page copy in `src/content/`. Expanding those (see the case-study and
testimonial issues) adds depth without creating a publishing obligation.

If an article does get written, publish it somewhere that doesn't need a
cadence to look healthy (a guest post, LinkedIn) and link to it from the
relevant page here.
